description: Write a specific, non-generic cover letter for one job
argument-hint: <application folder name>
---
Folder: `applications/$ARGUMENTS/`

Read `posting.md`, `evaluation.md`, the tailored `cv.md` if present, `profile/stories.md`, and `profile/preferences.md`.

Write `applications/$ARGUMENTS/cover-letter.md`, max ~300 words, in the posting's language:
- Opening line: something specific about this company/role, not "I am writing to apply for".
- 2 short paragraphs, each built on one real STAR story mapped to a key requirement.
- Address the biggest gap from the evaluation honestly and briefly (adjacent experience, how you'd close it).
- Close with a concrete next step.

Banned: "passionate", "team player", "I believe I would be a great fit", "dynamic", any claim not backed by `profile/`.
Then give a 1-line critique of the letter's weakest sentence and a stronger alternative.
